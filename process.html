How we work

Five clear steps, honest timelines, and you approve everything before it goes live. No black boxes.

  1. 1. Discovery — we listen first

    A relaxed call or chat about your business: what you sell, who your customers are, what you want the website to achieve. We also look at competitors and collect your content — logo, photos, prices, timings.

    You do: a 20–30 minute conversation. We do: notes, a project brief, and a fixed quote if anything custom is needed.

    Day 1–2

  2. 2. Design preview — see it before we build it

    We design the homepage first and share it with you as a visual preview. You request changes — colors, layout, wording — until it feels right. Nothing gets built until you approve the direction.

    You do: review and give feedback. We do: revisions until the design is approved.

    Day 3–5

  3. 3. Build — we develop everything

    All pages are developed: responsive layouts, contact forms, WhatsApp integration, Google Maps, and — depending on your package — the store, payments, chatbot or automations. We test on real phones and browsers as we go.

    You do: nothing — or send any missing content. We do: the full build plus internal testing.

    Day 6–11

  4. 4. Review & revisions — your turn to nitpick

    You get a private preview link and click through everything like a customer would. Send us every change you want — text tweaks, photo swaps, layout fixes. We refine until you're genuinely happy.

    You do: thorough review. We do: all reasonable revisions, included in the price.

    Day 12–13

  5. 5. Launch & support — go live, stay supported

    We connect your domain, publish the site, verify forms, payments and chatbot, and hand over everything: logins, files, and plain-language guides. Then we stay reachable — and care plans are available for ongoing help.

    You do: final payment (the remaining 50%). We do: launch, handover docs, and post-launch support.

    Day 14
