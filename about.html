About Nodrits Solutions

A small studio with a simple mission: give Pakistani businesses the same quality of web technology that big companies enjoy — at prices a small business can actually afford.

Our story

Technology shouldn't be a luxury

Too many small businesses in Pakistan run entirely on WhatsApp and Instagram — not because they don't want a proper website, but because agencies quote mysterious prices, disappear after launch, or build things the owner can't update.

Nodrits Solutions was founded to fix that. We publish our prices openly. We build mobile-first, WhatsApp-integrated websites with local payments baked in. And we stay after launch — because a website you can't maintain is a website that dies.

Our name comes from a simple idea: design, develop, automate, grow — the four things every business needs from technology, in the order they need them.

Founder

Nouman Asif

Web developer, designer and AI enthusiast based in Pakistan. Nouman builds SaaS products and intelligent workflows — and started Nodrits Solutions to bring that same practical technology to local businesses: websites that look world-class, AI assistants that answer customers around the clock, and automations that save hours every week.

He believes in showing work instead of making promises, fixed pricing instead of vague quotes, and honest timelines instead of "sir, ho jayega."
